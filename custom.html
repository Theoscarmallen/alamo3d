Before you ask

What's a lithophane? +
A thin white panel where the photo is carved into the thickness of the plastic. Looks like a plain white tile until you hold it up to a light or window, then the picture appears in full detail. Best with clear, high-contrast photos. The night-light version sits on a small USB LED base.
What's a photo lightbox? +
A small framed box with your photo split into layers and a light inside. Off, it looks like a clean shelf piece. Switched on, the picture glows with real depth. Runs off USB, so any phone charger powers it. Best with a clear photo where the subject stands out from the background.
What photo should I send? +
One clear, well-lit shot, the bigger the better. High contrast (a bright subject on a plainer background) comes out best on lithophanes, lightboxes, and reliefs. For keychains and flexi toys, a simple image or logo with clean edges works better than a busy photo. Not sure yours will work? Send it anyway and I'll tell you before printing.
Can I get it in more than one color? +
Single color is the flat price. Two-tone signs and multicolor pieces are doable, they just get a custom quote. Say what you want in the notes and I'll message you a number.
Do I get charged when I hit send? +
No. Sending shows you the price and the Cash App / Venmo buttons with the amount pre-filled. You pay there, with your order ID in the note, and that starts it. Everything is paid upfront, Cash App or Venmo only.
How long does it take? +
Most items are ready in 3 to 5 days from payment. Lightboxes and night lights take a little longer, 4 to 7 days. Rush jumps the queue. Pickup is in north central San Antonio, or $8 flat shipping anywhere in the US.
Something not on the list? +
Pick the closest thing and explain in the notes, or just email orders@alamo3dprinting.com with what you're picturing. If it's printable, I'll quote it.
