Before you ask

How does "photo to figure" actually work? +
I run your photo through an AI model generator (MakerWorld's Image to 3D and similar tools), clean up the result, and print it in one color. It captures the shape, pose, and vibe well. It won't nail every facial detail like a sculptor would. I send you a preview render before printing, and if it's not close enough, you get a refund and we call it a day.
What's a lithophane? +
A thin white panel where the photo is carved into the thickness of the plastic. Looks like a plain white tile until you hold it up to a light or window, then the picture appears in full detail. Best with clear, high-contrast photos. The night-light version sits on a small USB LED base.
Why is the AI stuff more expensive than a sign or a keychain? +
The generator tools cost me credits per model, plus there's cleanup and a preview round with you. Signs, keychains, lithophanes, cutters, and vases are built with free makers, so those are cheaper.
Can I get it in more than one color? +
Single color is the flat price. Two-tone signs and multicolor pieces are doable, they just get a custom quote. Say what you want in the notes and I'll message you a number.
Do I get charged when I hit send? +
No. Sending shows you the price and the Cash App / Venmo buttons with the amount pre-filled. You pay there, with your order ID in the note, and that starts it. Everything is paid upfront, Cash App or Venmo only.
How long does it take? +
Photo items need a modeling step first, so figure 4 to 7 days from payment (rush is 2 to 3). Signs, keychains, cutters, and ornaments are usually 3 to 5 days. Pickup is in north central San Antonio, or $8 flat shipping anywhere in the US.
Something not on the list? +
Pick the closest thing and explain in the notes, or just email orders@alamo3dprinting.com with what you're picturing. If it's printable, I'll quote it.
