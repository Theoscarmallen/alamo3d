Before you ask

What's a model link / STL? +
A model is the 3D file I print from. You don't need the file itself, just the link to its page on a site like Printables or MakerWorld. Search what you want, copy the page URL, paste it above. If there's no model, there's nothing to print, so I can't quote it.
Why does one color cost less than multicolor? +
The color doesn't cost more, black or hot pink is the same price. What costs more is the printer swapping between colors mid-print: it takes longer and wastes filament every swap. So single-color is a flat price, and multicolor gets a custom quote.
What material do you print in? +
Listed prices are for PLA+ and PETG, which covers almost everything: figures, props, desk stuff, functional parts. PLA+ is the default. PETG if it needs to be tougher or live in a hot car. Want something more exotic (TPU, ABS/ASA, carbon fiber, silk, whatever)? Ask, most of it's doable, it just gets quoted separately based on what that material needs to print right.
Can I just paint it myself? +
Absolutely, and it's cheaper. Order it in one color (white or gray primes best) and paint the details yourself. Lots of people do this for figures.
Do you do custom design work? +
Mostly I print existing models you find and link. If you've got your own file or a true custom idea, send it over anyway, I'll take a look, but custom design runs higher and takes longer.
How's the price figured? +
Flat by size for standard single-color prints. Really big or complex ones (over ~20 hrs of print time) I confirm with a custom number so nobody gets surprised. You'll always okay the final price before I start.
